# 💼 Business Financial Dashboard | Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 1. Project Overview

The **Business Financial Dashboard** is a business intelligence project developed to analyze financial performance, sales trends, product profitability, regional performance, payment methods, and sales representative contributions.

The dashboard consolidates key performance indicators (KPIs) into an interactive report, allowing users to monitor business results and explore financial performance across different months, product categories, regions, and sales representatives.

The project demonstrates how data analytics and dashboard visualization can help transform business data into meaningful insights for decision-making.

## 🎯 2. Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor total revenue, total profit, order volume, and profit margin.
- Analyze monthly sales trends and changes in performance.
- Compare net profit across geographical regions.
- Identify the products contributing most to profitability.
- Examine revenue distribution by payment method.
- Evaluate sales representative performance.
- Use interactive filters to explore financial results.
- Provide business recommendations based on data-driven analysis.

## 🧮 6. DAX Measures

The following DAX formulas are templates for your dashboard. They assume a data table named `Sales` with columns such as `Revenue`, `Profit`, `Order ID`, `Region`, `Product`, `Payment Method`, `Sales Representative`, `Month`, and `Discount Amount`.

**Replace the table and column names with the exact names in your Power BI model.** If your actual table is named differently, replace `Sales` throughout the formulas.

### 6.1 Total Revenue

```dax
Total Revenue =
SUM ( Sales[Revenue] )
```

**Explanation:** Adds the revenue values in the current filter context.

**Used for:** Total Revenue KPI, monthly sales trend, revenue by payment method, and sales representative performance.

### 6.2 Total Profit

```dax
Total Profit =
SUM ( Sales[Profit] )
```

**Explanation:** Adds the profit values in the dataset.

**Used for:** Total Profit KPI and profit analysis by region and product.

### 6.3 Total Orders

If each order has a unique order ID:

```dax
Total Orders =
DISTINCTCOUNT ( Sales[Order ID] )
```

**Explanation:** Counts unique orders rather than individual transaction rows.

**Used for:** Total Orders KPI.

If each row is guaranteed to represent one unique order, `COUNTROWS(Sales)` is an alternative. Use the formula that matches your data structure.

### 6.4 Profit Margin (%)

```dax
Profit Margin (%) =
DIVIDE (
    [Total Profit],
    [Total Revenue],
    0
)
```

**Explanation:** Calculates profit as a proportion of revenue.

**Formatting:** Select the measure and set its format to Percentage.

**Used for:** Profit Margin KPI and product profitability analysis.

### 6.5 Net Profit by Region

```dax
Net Profit by Region =
[Total Profit]
```

**Explanation:** Reuses the total profit measure. The Region field in the visual determines the regional result.

**Visual setup:**
- Y-axis: Region
- X-axis or Values: Net Profit by Region

### 6.6 Monthly Sales Trend

```dax
Monthly Sales =
[Total Revenue]
```

**Explanation:** Calculates sales for each month based on the visual's filter context.

**Visual setup:**
- X-axis: Month or Date
- Y-axis: Monthly Sales

For correct chronological ordering, use a proper date field or sort the month name by a month-number column.

### 6.7 Revenue by Payment Method

```dax
Revenue by Payment Method =
[Total Revenue]
```

**Explanation:** Calculates revenue for each payment method when the visual groups the measure by payment method.

**Visual setup:**
- Legend: Payment Method
- Values: Revenue by Payment Method

### 6.8 Net Profit by Product

```dax
Net Profit by Product =
[Total Profit]
```

**Explanation:** Calculates profit for each product in the current filter context.

**Visual setup:**
- Y-axis: Product
- X-axis or Values: Net Profit by Product

### 6.9 Product Discount Amount

```dax
Total Discount Amount =
SUM ( Sales[Discount Amount] )
```

**Explanation:** Adds the discount amounts recorded for transactions.

**Used for:** The Top 7 Products table.

Confirm whether the dataset stores discounts as monetary amounts or percentages. If the source field is a percentage, this formula may not represent the actual discount value.

### 6.10 Product Revenue

```dax
Product Revenue =
[Total Revenue]
```

**Explanation:** Reuses the revenue measure to calculate revenue by product in the table or chart.

### 6.11 Product Profit Margin (%)

```dax
Product Profit Margin (%) =
DIVIDE (
    [Total Profit],
    [Total Revenue],
    0
)
```

**Explanation:** Calculates profit margin for each product in the current filter context.

**Important:** Format this measure as a percentage. Do not multiply by 100 in the formula if Power BI is already formatting the result as a percentage.

### 6.12 Revenue by Sales Representative

```dax
Revenue by Sales Representative =
[Total Revenue]
```

**Explanation:** Calculates revenue for each sales representative when grouped by the representative field.

**Visual setup:**
- Axis: Sales Representative
- Values: Revenue by Sales Representative
- Optional filter: Top N = 7, if you want to display the seven highest-performing representatives.

### 6.13 Previous Month Revenue

For month-over-month comparison, create a dedicated Calendar table with a continuous Date column related to the sales table.

```dax
Previous Month Revenue =
CALCULATE (
    [Total Revenue],
    DATEADD (
        'Calendar'[Date],
        -1,
        MONTH
    )
)
```

### 6.14 Revenue Change (%)

```dax
Revenue Change (%) =
DIVIDE (
    [Total Revenue] - [Previous Month Revenue],
    [Previous Month Revenue],
    0
)
```

**Explanation:** Measures the percentage change in revenue compared with the previous month.

### 6.15 Profit Change (%)

```dax
Previous Month Profit =
CALCULATE (
    [Total Profit],
    DATEADD (
        'Calendar'[Date],
        -1,
        MONTH
    )
)
```

```dax
Profit Change (%) =
DIVIDE (
    [Total Profit] - [Previous Month Profit],
    [Previous Month Profit],
    0
)
```

These measures can be used for month-over-month KPI comparisons, provided the Calendar table and date relationships are configured correctly.

## 💡 8. Business Recommendations

These recommendations should be validated against the underlying dataset before being presented as final findings.

### Improve Profitability

Monitor profit margin alongside revenue. Increasing revenue does not necessarily mean profitability has improved.

### Review Regional Performance

Investigate regional differences in profit by examining revenue, costs, product mix, and discounting. Use the results to identify areas where performance could improve.

### Optimize Product Performance

Compare products using both revenue and profit margin. Prioritize decisions based on profitability, customer demand, and inventory requirements rather than revenue alone.

### Evaluate Discounting

Review discount amounts alongside product margins to determine whether discounts are supporting sales without unnecessarily reducing profit.

### Monitor Monthly Sales

Investigate significant monthly declines and increases. Use historical patterns to support sales planning, staffing, and inventory decisions.

### Review Payment Channels

Compare revenue across payment methods and consider transaction fees, payment reliability, and customer convenience before changing payment options.

### Support Sales Representatives

Use representative-level revenue as one performance indicator. Consider sales targets, customer portfolios, margins, and territory differences before making performance comparisons.

### Establish Regular KPI Monitoring

Review revenue, profit, order volume, and margin consistently. Ensure that measures use consistent definitions and reporting periods.

## ⚠️ 9. Project Limitations

- **Data Quality:** Missing, duplicated, or inaccurate records can affect financial calculations.
- **Metric Definitions:** Revenue, profit, discount amount, and order count must be defined consistently.
- **Profit Margin Accuracy:** Product margins should be validated to ensure profit is divided by revenue and the result is formatted correctly.
- **Historical Analysis:** The dashboard describes recorded performance and does not automatically predict future results.
- **Filter Context:** KPI values may change when users select different months or categories.
- **Sales Representative Comparisons:** Revenue alone does not account for differences in sales targets, customer portfolios, or profit margins.
- **Payment Method Analysis:** Revenue distribution does not necessarily reflect transaction fees or payment-processing costs.
- **Example DAX:** The formulas must be adapted to the actual table and column names before use.
- **Screenshot Values:** The values shown in the screenshot should be reconciled with the final Power BI report before publication.
